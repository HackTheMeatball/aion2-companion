## 1. Goals and non-goals

**Goals (ROADMAP 0.4).** 0.3 made Next up *correct* (the evaluator answers "open?" and "done?"); 0.4 makes it *smart*
by changing only the **order and the advice**, never what is open:

1. **F15, location-aware:** equal choices are tie-broken by distance from the player's last confirmed location
   (derived from change times, never stored).
2. **F10, two tracks:** "fastest" (default, owner 2026-10-14) and "completionist", from the data's `route` field;
   level bands shown; confirmed drawbacks of "fastest" shown next to the toggle.
3. **F1, catch-up:** "what can I do now with my level and points", for a player whose level is ahead of their ticks.
4. **Travel advice:** Kibelisk binding derived from ticks; a one-time "Bind X" row; teleport vs walk.
5. **Ascension first** (owner, 2026-10-14; 0.3 invariant 3 is still PENDING, 0.3 §12): a met Ascension step is the
   first step card. 0.3 §8 (the Next up row) states Ascension steps "rank first", but the same row keeps "ranking order
   unchanged in 0.3" and §12 leaves the invariant PENDING, so the ranking change was deferred; 0.4 enforces it.

**Non-goals.** No new gate, no change to `open` / `done` (that is the evaluator's, 0.3). No live position tracking,
packet reading or screenshot parsing (CLAUDE.md). No scroll inventory (DECISIONS: scrolls are not tracked). No zone
adjacency graph (no source exists; §9). F9 (filler XP per minute) waits for the owner's measurements; §13 S9 reserves
the slot. Characters (0.5) and sync (0.6) are untouched, but everything here is derived from fields sync already merges.

## 2. Today's ranking (the baseline 0.4 changes)

`rankNext(state, { basic })` (`src/core/nextup.js:21`) builds a fixed sequence of tiers. Every open / done question goes
through `gatesOf(c)` (the evaluator, since 0.3 E2); the order is 0.2's:

| Tier | Cards | Where location is used today |
|---|---|---|
| 1 spend | `run` (dungeon in progress), `cube`, `skill` (+ up to 3 queued), `board` / `boardcost`, `stequip` / `stup` | none |
| 2 milestone you stand on | `step` (non-regional, non-advice steps of `here`) | none |
| 3 story level-gated | `rq` then `sd` move above the story when `gated` (`storyRecGate`) | none |
| 4 missed | first 2 non-regional `missed` steps | none |
| 5 story | `story` (one card) | `storyHereOf` names the next Kibelisk (`nextup.js:154`) |
| 6 side content | `rq`, `sd` (when not gated), `stwait`, `adv` (≤ 3), `sh` | `hereArea` = **zone of the story step** (`storyAreaOf(storyHereOf(state), KIB_AREA)`, `nextup.js:61`): the SD pick prefers it (`:64`), the SH pick too (`:107`), the rq card counts quests `near` it (`:90`) |
| 7 later | `laterSkill` (≤ 3), `laterStep` (next milestone), sorted by level | none |

Basic drops later, advice and queued skill cards; at most 8 cards plus the story and side-content cards (`:125–129`).

Three facts from the code that this design builds on or corrects:

- **`lastConfirmedLocation(c)`** (`src/core/sync.js:178`) exists but **nothing on the page calls it** (only
  `tests/core/sync.test.mjs`). It reads only `mt.checks` stamps with time > 0, and its locator `zoneOfKey` knows only
  Sealed Dungeons, strongholds and regional quests (`sync.js:170`). Story objective ticks (`mt.qsteps`), the story
  position (`mt.quest`), and regional pick-ups (`mt.started`) never move it. For a player who mostly follows the story,
  it is almost always `null` or stale.
- **`Ev.node()` carries no `route` and no zone.** 0.3 §14 says `Ev.list()` gives them; it does not: GATE_GRAPH nodes
  are `{kind, key, req, done, …}` (`tools/gen/gen.mjs:441–464`) and the evaluator result is
  `{id, kind, key, done, via?, open, blockers, root, info, basis, eta}` (`evaluator.js:123`). `eta` exists. This
  design keeps the evaluator gate-only and adds the place data beside it (§3), so GATE_GRAPH and its 0.3 equivalence
  checks stay byte-identical.
- **The golden master has no dated change times.** The 2,000 golden states and the 10,000 invariant states carry no
  `mt` with time > 0, so every location-aware rule is a no-op on them. 0.4 needs a new state family (§12).

## 3. The place layer

A compiled sidecar, `NODE_PLACE` (new generated module `src/data/node-place.js`, like `gate-graph.js`), keyed by the
same node ids as GATE_GRAPH:

```js
NODE_PLACE[id] = {
  route: "core" | "filler" | "completionist",     // record `route`; absent = core (story quests have none: core)
  zone: "zone:…" | null,                          // the zone the card sends you to (see "target")
  pts: { giver?, turnin?, entrance?, steps?: [] } // each point { map, x, y, kib, m } (Metabot; 1 px ≈ 1 m)
}
KIBELISKS[kibId] = { name, zone, map, pos?: { x, y, conf } }   // pos: derived (location research §5), never displayed (Q9, Q10)
```

- **Points** come from the data's existing `giver` / `turnin` / `entrance` / `location` blocks (`kibelisk`, `meters`,
  `coords`). Story **objective** points do not exist in the data yet: 240 of the 432 story objective lines carry a
  `(Kibelisk: X)` marker, **none** carries metres or coordinates (§9). S1 adds an optional `at {map, x, y, kibelisk,
  meters, sources}` to `storyQuests.steps[]` from the Metabot quest pages' step markers (the location research found
  185 step markers on 51 story quests; they were counted, not stored).
- **The target of a card** (what "distance to" means): story → the next open objective's point, else the turn-in;
  regional quest → giver if not picked up, else its turn-in; Sealed Dungeon / stronghold → entrance; plan / skill /
  stigma / Cube cards → none (no travel). Ascension nodes have no place (owner, 2026-10-14).
- **Kibelisk registry.** Kibelisks are names in the data today: **53** distinct names in story quests (giver /
  turn-in `kibelisk` fields plus `(Kibelisk: X)` objective markers), **69** once every `kibelisk` field of regional
  quests, Sealed Dungeons and strongholds is added (counted on this commit). The Kibelisk research
  (`docs/research/2026-10-kibelisks.md`) has **73**: Metabot's 64 listed names (61 Altgard, 3 Ishalgen) plus 9 that only
  quest pages cite; its 53 story Kibelisks equal ours, and the 4 extra are listed names no record of ours cites.
  `zones[].kibelisks` is `inferred` and disagrees with the quest data in places
  (`zones.json` lists "Safe Haven Cliff" as the Kibelisk and "Safe Haven" as a camp of Dredgion Crash Site, while 9
  story records name the Kibelisk "Safe Haven"). S1 adds a `kibelisks` collection with stable ids (`kib:<slug>`),
  each name every record uses resolving to one id (generator check). The bind key (§8) is built from that id. **The
  slug is frozen when the record is created**: renaming the Kibelisk in `zones.json` or in the collection changes
  `name`, never `id`, so a `bind:` key never moves (§10).
- **Derived positions** (`pos`) are the location research's trilaterated Kibelisk positions (39 of 60 recovered, 20
  high confidence). Used only to place the player when the last event is a Kibelisk with no point of its own (a bind
  tick, or a story objective tick before S1, Q9), never displayed (location research §7 recommendation; Q10), never
  used for a `low` confidence fit. Q9 and Q10 only narrow this rule; neither displays a position.

## 4. Where the player is

`whereNow(c)` (pure, `src/core/place.js`), extending `lastConfirmedLocation` without changing it (its tests stay):

1. Scan every dated stamp (time > 0) in `mt` that means "I was here":

   | Stamp | Place |
   |---|---|
   | `mt.checks[k] = [t, true]`, k a regional quest / SD / SH / step with a point | its target point when done (rq: turn-in; SD / SH: entrance) |
   | `mt.started[k] = [t, 1]` | regional quest giver |
   | `mt.qsteps[quest][i] = [t, true]` | objective i's point (after S1); before S1: its Kibelisk's `pos`, else its Kibelisk's zone (no point) |
   | `mt.quest = [t, [q, storyEnd]]` | quest q's giver (≤ 4 m from the previous turn-in on every same-map chain hop, location research §7); `storyEnd = q`: q's turn-in |
   | `mt.checks["bind:<kib>"] = [t, true]` | the Kibelisk (`pos`, else its zone) |
   | Ascension steps, `ascensionQuest` records | ignored (owner, 2026-10-14) |

2. The newest stamp wins; a time tie breaks on the item key, larger key wins (as `lastConfirmedLocation` does:
   `t === best.t && k > best.key`, `src/core/sync.js:178–190`), so every device derives the same answer after `merge()`.
   Stamps outside `checks` (`started`, `qsteps`, `quest`) get a key of their own for this (`started:<k>`,
   `qsteps:<quest>:<i>`, `quest`), fixed in S2's tests.
3. Result: `{ via, t, zone, pt: {map, x, y} | null }`, or `null` (no dated stamp: fall back to today's `hereArea`, the
   zone of the story step; with neither, today's order).

*[inferred]* A tick says "I was there when I ticked", not "I am there now"; the player may have teleported since.
That is why location is a **tie-break**, never a tier change (§5), and why the travel line always names its start
("from VeKauf").

## 5. Location-aware ranking (F15)

**Rule.** The tiers of §2 stay in their order. Location only reorders or picks **inside** a tier, with one comparator:

```js
byPlace(here)(a, b) =
     sameZone(a) - sameZone(b)            // in here.zone first (0) else 1
  || dist(here.pt, a.pt) - dist(here.pt, b.pt)   // straight line, same map only; unknown or other map = Infinity
  || a.catalogIndex - b.catalogIndex     // today's order: stable
```

Candidates come from `Ev.list(n => n.open && !n.done)` joined with `NODE_PLACE[n.id]`; nodes the evaluator closes
never reach the comparator (invariant 1).

| Place in `rankNext` | Today | With `placeRank` on |
|---|---|---|
| `rqFirst` (the card's "go" target) and the order of `rqOpen` | first fresh (not picked up, not locked) quest in catalog order; list in catalog order | same preference (fresh, then unlocked, then any), each by `byPlace`; list by `byPlace`, follow-ups kept under their parent |
| `sdPick` | highest open band; story-step zone first | highest open band; then `byPlace` (story-step zone when `here` is null) |
| `shPick` | story-step zone, else `lo` desc, `hi` asc | `byPlace`, then `lo` desc, `hi` asc |
| `rqCard.near` | open rq in the story-step zone | open rq in `here.zone` |
| missed steps (first 2) | catalog order | unchanged: steps have no point (only `g20:exp`-like rows have a zone) |
| story card | — | unchanged (the story is linear) |

Band before distance for Sealed Dungeons is deliberate: the band is XP and rewards; distance is a tie-break
(DECISIONS "tie-break equal choices"). **Ascension first** (`ascensionFirst` switch): a met, not-done Ascension step
moves to the top of tier 2 and above tier 1 (DATA-FORMAT-V2 §10, owner); 0.3 invariant 3 then flips from PENDING to
enforced.

**Zone adjacency** is not used: no zone has `neighbours` (0 of 27 in `zones.json`) and no source for them is in the repo.
Straight-line metres on the Altgard map stand in *[inferred: a good proxy within Altgard because Kibelisk teleports
make most trips cheap; it ignores terrain]*.

## 6. Fastest / completionist (F10)

**Where the labels are today** (counted from `data/**`):

| Records | `route` |
|---|---|
| guide steps (53) | 26 `core`, 7 `completionist` (`g20:exp`, `g25:0`, `g35:exp`, `g45:2`–`g45:5`: today's optional rows), 20 unset (= core) |
| regional quests (58), Sealed Dungeons (60), strongholds (15) | all `core` (the 5b default "`optional` → completionist, else core"; never reviewed by the owner) |
| story quests (86) | none (the story is the track) |
| `filler` anywhere | 0 |
| milestone `drawbacks` | 0 of 21 milestones |

So a toggle built today would change almost nothing. Design:

- **Semantics.** *Fastest*: cards whose candidates are all non-`core` are dropped, **except when the story is
  level-gated** (`storyRecGate`), where filler is how you catch up XP (today's tier 3 rule, kept). *Completionist*:
  every route ranks as today's `core`, and `completionist` steps (today's optional rows, which only `adv` steps reach)
  are offered after the story. With today's labels, fastest = today's output exactly (golden identical); every
  difference comes from a data label change, each one attributed by `--explain` (§12).
- **Labels are data.** Data & Research proposes labels per milestone (`filler` for regional quests and Sealed Dungeons
  that give nothing the core path needs; `completionist` for collection-only content); the owner approves per
  milestone (DECISIONS 2026-10-14). A record whose skipping loses something gets a `drawbacks` entry on its milestone
  (verified sources only; e.g. a regional quest with a unique reward).
- **Toggle.** A view setting `track` (`"fastest"` default, `"completionist"`), top-level like `mode`; never synced
  (DECISIONS: view settings don't sync). Shown in the Next up header next to the drawbacks line ("Fastest skips: Cube
  Expansion Voucher (Shugo-of-All-Trades)"). **Hidden until at least one milestone has an approved non-core label**: a
  toggle that changes nothing is noise (Q7). **This deviates from DECISIONS "0.3 default mode"** ("Next up defaults
  to 'fastest', with a 'completionist' toggle"), which promises a visible toggle; it needs the owner's OK (Q7). Without
  it, the toggle ships visible in S5 and is a no-op until labels change.
- **Level bands** (F10 "grouped by level band with the range shown"): when Next up holds cards from more than one
  milestone band (missed + here + later), one small band label per group ("Lv 20–24"), bands = the milestone levels.
  No label when everything is in one band. Mockup first (§13 S5).

## 7. Catch-up (F1)

"What can I do now with my level and points", for the player whose level ran ahead of their ticks (joined mid-level,
forgot to tick, levelled on filler).

**When.** `behind(c) = missedOpen ≥ 3 || spendable ≥ 2`, where `missedOpen` counts open, not-done steps of milestones
below the level, and `spendable` counts kinds with something affordable now: skill points ≥ next open line's cost,
board points ≥ the next node's logged or minimum cost, shards ≥ the next stigma upgrade. *[inferred thresholds; tuned
on the golden families, §12]*

**What changes** (`catchUp` switch; no new mode, no new card type):

1. **Spend queue lifted:** today the queued skill cards stop once Next up holds 4 cards (`nextup.js:36`) and missed
   steps at 2 (`:98`). When
   behind, the queue covers everything the current points afford (in the milestones' order, as today), and the missed
   steps all show, grouped by band (§6). Basic keeps its rule (first skill card only).
2. **Ranked by "now":** within tier 4 the missed steps sort by `byPlace` (§5) then catalog order, not guide order.
3. **Later by `eta`:** the tail's "Later" cards come from `Ev.list(n => !n.open && !n.done)` sorted by
   `eta.level` (then `eta.quest` position), so a skill line that opens at Lv 27 and a milestone step that waits on a
   quest are interleaved by when they open; the blocker text is 0.3 E6's.

Points, crystals and shards are what the app already tracks (`skillPoints`, `dvLeft`, `stShards`); nothing new is
asked from the player. The fuller "joining mid-level" flow (set level and quest, earlier content counted as done) is
0.7.

## 8. Travel advice: binding, nudge, teleport vs walk

### 8.1 Binding (derived from ticks, never stored)

Owner (2026-10-15, 19:20 ET): binding happens on proximity, observed at about 30–40 m; the app assumes "bound" only
within **25 m**; teleport works from the first bind; a teleport costs a Return Scroll. The 25 m figure is an **open
decision** (§8.4): it is the working value, read from `rule:kibelisk-bind-radius`, never a constant in code.

```
bound(K) =  ∃ visited point P with P.kib = K and P.m ≤ R          // R = rule:kibelisk-bind-radius (working value 25 m, §8.4)
         ∨  checks["bind:" + K]                                  // the player ticked the nudge row (§8.2)
         ∨  ∃ visited story objective with binds = K             // owner-observed bind facts (data, basis owner)
```

**Visited** (never early; all from ticks, the story position and pick-ups):

| Point | Visited when |
|---|---|
| story quest i giver | quest i turned in, or any objective of i ticked |
| story quest i turn-in | quest i turned in (`pos > i`, or `pos = i` with `storyEnd`) |
| story objective i.j (after S1) | objective ticked, or quest i turned in |
| regional quest giver / turn-in | picked up or done / done (tick or derived) |
| SD / SH entrance | done (tick or derived) or `areaRun` |

`P.m` is Metabot's stated nearest-Kibelisk distance on that point, so "within R" needs no position.

**What the 25 m rule gives on today's data** (giver and turn-in points only, counted per Kibelisk name): of 53
Kibelisks in the story data, **22** are bound by some story giver / turn-in within 25 m, **15** only ever come within
25–150 m, and **16** have no giver / turn-in point within 150 m; 15 of those 16 appear **only** in objective markers,
so their distance is unknown, not large (S1's objective points decide them). With every `kibelisk` field of regional
quests, dungeons and strongholds added: 69 names, 34 / 25 / 10. Story giver / turn-in points at 25–40 m (where the
owner saw binding happen, but the rule says "not bound"): 23 of 163; how they relate to the research's eight 29–34 m
Kibelisks is in §8.4.

**The Safe Haven case breaks the 25 m rule** (one of two distance cases it gets wrong, §8.4). The owner's bind
happens on "Survival Supplies: Receive additional supplies" (go to VeKauf, `sq:2102015` objective 0). VeKauf (the
turn-in point) is **32 m** from the Safe Haven Kibelisk; the nearest point in all our data is 26 m (a regional quest).
So at 25 m the rule alone **never** binds Safe Haven, the main hub of Lv 10–14. *[inferred, from coordinates]* The
straight line from the giver Hans (3236, 2764; 96 m) to
VeKauf (3183, 2881) passes within ≈ 0 m of the recovered Safe Haven position (3196, 2852; high confidence): the
player walks over the Kibelisk on that objective. Hence the third clause: an optional **`binds: "kib:…"`** on a story
objective, `basis: owner` (or `sourced`), compiled as a fact. It is needed whatever R the owner picks (a fact beats a
radius), but how many `binds` facts are needed depends on R. Two to set now: Survival Supplies objective 0 → Safe
Haven (owner observation), and A Village in the Fog objective 1 "Remember Aldelle Village — bind with the Kibelisk"
→ Aldelle Village (**step text**: a scripted bind step). Aldelle Village and Witch's Cabin ("The Being Beneath the
Lake" opens with "Bind with the Witch's Cabin Kibelisk") are **scripted binds**: the quest tells the player to bind,
so these observations **say nothing about distance** and are not evidence for or against any R (Kibelisk research,
§Owner evidence, cases 1 and 2; Witch's Cabin is "inconclusive" there and has an open owner question). They support
the `binds` clause only. More facts come from owner checks (§14 Q1). A "path passes within R" rule from derived
positions is possible (Q1, option C) but is not the default.

### 8.2 The "Bind X" nudge (one-time, a tick)

- **When:** the story card's target point T (§3) has `R < T.m ≤ 150` (R as in §8.1), K = T.kib is not bound, and T
  is the **first point in story order** with Kibelisk K in that band. "One-time" is therefore derived from the data,
  not stored: once the player passes that point, the row is gone for good (the R rule or a later `binds` fact may
  still bind K). Known failure: a Kibelisk the player binds on the way to T can still get the row (§8.4, Galorik's).
- **What:** one row on the story card (not a card of its own), e.g. "Bind Safe Haven on the way · 32 m from VeKauf",
  with a tick box. Key `bind:<kib id>` in `checks` (permanent, never reused; §10). Ticking it binds K and moves
  `whereNow` to K.
- **Beyond 150 m:** nothing.
- **Scope in 0.4:** story targets only (linear, so "first" is well defined). Regional and entrance targets: Q2.

### 8.3 Teleport vs walk

For a card with a target T and a start `here.pt` (§4):

```
walk = here.pt and T on the same map ? |here.pt − T| : unknown          // straight line (location research §4)
tele = bound(T.kib) ? T.m : unknown                                     // teleport to T.kib, then run T.m
advise teleport  iff  !preferRun && !walkThisCard && tele known && (walk unknown ? here.zone ≠ T.zone : walk − tele ≥ TELEPORT_GAIN)
```

- **Shown** as one line on that card: "Teleport to Safe Haven (1 scroll), then 32 m". Walking is the default and
  shows nothing (minimal distraction). The line has an **"I'll walk"** button; the line disappears for that target.
- **`TELEPORT_GAIN`:** starts at **400 m** (DECISIONS 2026-10-14 example, "tuned in game"); a `rule:` record in
  `rules.json` so it is data, not code.
- **"Prefer running"** view setting (`preferRun`, off by default): no teleport line at all.
- **Cost:** "1 scroll" only; no Kinah figure (DECISIONS: ~1,000 Kinah, not sourced per patch), no inventory.
- **No line** when T has no point, T is an Ascension node, the maps differ and the zone is the same, or the Kibelisk
  is not bound (then the nudge of §8.2 may show instead).
- Displayed metres are always Metabot's stated `m`; walk distances are used to decide, never printed (location
  research §7).

### 8.4 Open decision: Kibelisk bind threshold

**Not decided by this design.** The owner chose **25 m** (DECISIONS "Kibelisk binding (0.4)", 2026-10-15: "0.4
assumes 'bound' only within 25 m of a quest step; 25–150 m gets a one-time 'Bind X' nudge"). The Kibelisk research
(`docs/research/2026-10-kibelisks.md`, Data & Research, merged in #83) finds that **the data fit a bound threshold of
at least 32 m** on step endpoints, and that on the three owner observations that test distance **the 25 m rule is
right once, wrong twice**. 25 m is therefore only the **working value** until the owner decides; the CEO will propose
about 35 m at the 0.4 review. The threshold is a data record, `rule:kibelisk-bind-radius` in `rules.json` (S1), next to
`rule:kibelisk-nudge-radius` (150) and `rule:teleport-gain` (400), so changing it is a one-line data PR plus a
golden `--explain` table; no code reads a literal 25.

**Evidence** (all from the Kibelisk research, §Summary and §Owner evidence; 5 owner observations on one alt character):

| Case | Owner saw | Nearest story points | 25 m rule says | Research verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Abandoned Barracks Site | unlocked on turning in "Signs of a Crash Landing" | turn-in Garlond 9 m | bound | agrees |
| Safe Haven | bound during "Survival Supplies" (VeKauf) | step 1 and turn-in 32 m; earlier points 96 m | nudge | **wrong** |
| Galorik's Inspection Area | bound while running to "Crash Site Rendezvous" step 1 | step 1 (Hadala) 50 m, turn-in 49 m | nudge | **wrong** |
| Aldelle Village | bound in quest 1 | giver 23 m, turn-in 4 m; explicit bind step | bound | scripted bind: says nothing about distance |
| Witch's Cabin | bound early in "The Being Beneath the Lake" | 12–14 m in "Isolated Lake"; explicit bind step | bound one quest earlier | scripted bind; inconclusive (owner question open) |

- **Eight story Kibelisks sit at 29–34 m** (nearest known quest point): Safe Haven 32, Quai Campsite 30, Minushan Site
  34, Faded Stump 29, Graverobber Campsite 30, Steel Hammer Merchants HQ 33, Steel Hammer Workcamp 33, Collapsed Hall
  of Fame 32. At 25 m all eight are "nudge"; inside the owner's observed 30–40 m they are bound. A further four sit
  at 46–49 m (Galorik's 49, Uruthumheim Cliff 46, Briskwind Shelter 49, Latesran Eastern Root 49).
- **Reconciling "23 of 163 points at 25–40 m" (§8.1) with the research's 8.** The units differ. The 23 are story
  **points** (giver / turn-in only, the 163 with metres in our data; 25 < m ≤ 40); they name **10** Kibelisks, several
  points per Kibelisk. Three of the 10 are bound elsewhere at ≤ 25 m (Aldelle Village 4 m, Zemurru's Tomb 24 m,
  Shulak Street Stall 25 m) and drop out. The other 7 match the research's list, with Graverobber Campsite at 36 m on
  our points but 30 m on a step the research read from a Metabot page. The research's eighth, Steel Hammer Merchants HQ
  (33 m), comes from a step point only (giver / turn-in nearest is not in that band). The research also counted
  **Kibelisks** by their nearest point over giver, turn-in **and** the steps of the 17 quest pages it fetched, in a
  29–34 m band. Both counts say the same thing: the 25–40 m gap is real and holds the Lv 10–14 hub.

## 11. Invariants (extends the `invariant` suite)

0.3's six invariants keep holding (0.4 changes only the order). New, each with its state id and card on failure:

7. **No location, no change:** with every dated stamp removed (`mt` times set to 0), `rankNext` with the 0.4 switches
   on equals `rankNext` with them off, except `ascensionFirst` and `catchUp` (which do not read location).
8. **Location only reorders inside a tier:** for any two `here` values, the sequence of card kinds is the same; only
   the pick and the order inside `rq` / `sd` / `sh` / missed differ.
9. **Ascension first** (0.3 invariant 3, enforced): a met, not-done Ascension step is the first step card in every
   state.
10. **Tracks nest:** every card and every candidate in "fastest" is in "completionist"; with today's labels the two
    differ only by `completionist` steps.
11. **Binding is monotonic in progress:** ticking more, picking up, or moving the story on never unbinds a Kibelisk;
    moving the story back or un-ticking may.
12. **Nudge:** shown only for `R < m ≤ 150` (both read from the rule records), never for a Kibelisk bound by the data
    (radius, tick or `binds`), at most one per Kibelisk across all story positions, never beyond 150 m. It cannot catch
    a bind the data doesn't record (Galorik's, §8.4); the `bind` golden family pins that case instead.
13. **Teleport line:** never with `preferRun`, never for an unbound Kibelisk, never across maps on a distance claim,
    never on an Ascension node; the printed metres equal the data's `m`.
14. **Deterministic after merge:** `whereNow(merge(a, b)) = whereNow(merge(b, a))` on the sync suite's pairs.
15. **Nothing written:** `whereNow`, `bound`, `rankNext` run on deep-frozen saves (0.3 invariant 6 extended).

The oracle (`tests/support/oracle.mjs`) gets an independent `bound` / `whereNow` written from this section over the raw
JSON, as 0.3 did for gates.

## 15. Summary for the owner

0.4 doesn't change what the guide considers open or done; that stays 0.3's job. It changes the order and adds travel
help. Next up will prefer things near where you last ticked something. It will also add a "fastest" track (default)
and a "completionist" track, and a catch-up view that lists everything your level and points already allow. Kibelisks
count as bound when your progress took you within a set distance of them; nothing new is saved for this. When a
Kibelisk is farther than that but within 150 m of where the story sends you, you get a one-time "Bind X" row to tick.
A teleport line ("Teleport to X, 1 scroll, then N m", with an "I'll walk" button) appears only when it saves a real
distance, starting at 400 m.

**One decision is yours: that set distance.** You chose 25 m. The Kibelisk research checked your five observations
against our data: on the three that test distance, 25 m was right once and wrong twice. Safe Haven bound at 32 m, and
Galorik's Inspection Area bound while you ran to a point 50 m away. Eight story Kibelisks sit at 29–34 m, inside the
30–40 m you saw, so 25 m would show a "Bind" row for each of them. The research finds the data fit at least 32 m; the
CEO will propose about 35 m at the 0.4 review. Until you decide, 25 m is the working value. It is a single data
setting, so changing it later is easy. Aldelle Village and Witch's Cabin don't count either way: those quests tell you
to bind. Whatever you pick, the design also records your Survival Supplies observation (Safe Haven) as a fact, and
you'd be asked to confirm a few more from a short in-game list. One known gap: when you bind a Kibelisk on the way
(as at Galorik's), the "Bind X" row may still show once; ticking it clears it.

Before any of this is visible, we need Metabot's per-objective map points (none are loaded yet) and a
Kibelisk list with fixed ids. Then you approve each visible step with before/after screenshots, as in 0.3. The
fastest/completionist toggle stays hidden until you've approved real labels, because with today's data it would change
almost nothing. That differs from your earlier "fastest, with a completionist toggle" decision, so it needs your OK.
