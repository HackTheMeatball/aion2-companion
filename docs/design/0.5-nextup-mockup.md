full width, 44 px, `--accent-fill`; later cards compact, a number tile, a title and one line; "1 of N" replaces the nested
scroll stack and shows in Advanced only, because Basic hides `#nowStar .ns-head`). 1500 px frames draw the card as the
third top tile (as the app does today) with the desktop green action button; the page's other tiles are dashed

**Where things live.** Advanced: a header (title, "1 of N", "i") and under it one options row: the track toggle (when
shown), "Prefer running", and the drawbacks line below them. Rows that belong to one card (Bind, travel line) sit inside
that card, under its text and above its action, separated by a hairline, so they read as part of the card and don't
add a card. Basic: no header, so no toggle, no band labels, no "Prefer running", no pager; the Bind row and the travel
line show as one row each on the card Basic already shows (Q3). Rows use 44 px tap rows on phones (the whole Bind row
is the tick target; "I'll walk" is 44 px).

**No redundancy.** The rows don't repeat Journey or the milestone cards: Bind and travel talk only about getting to this
card's target; band labels are the milestone levels the cards already belong to, shown once per group; the drawbacks
line names what the track skips, which no other card says. The teleport line replaces the Kibelisk hint, never sits
beside it.

**Assumption to confirm (Q8 below):** on a phone, band labels sit above the first card and above each group of compact
cards; the pager steps the featured card through the same order.
