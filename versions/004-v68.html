      How skill points work

      You earn points two ways: from character levels (24 by Lv 12, 28 by Lv 13, up to 203 at Lv 45), and 1 per Wisdom Stone you use from your Cube. Enter your stones used above and the card works out what you can spend. The skill window's points breakdown shows both and how many are unspent. Each skill gains one level every 3 character levels after you learn it. Levels 2–4 cost 1 point, 5–7 cost 2, 8–10 cost 4; points stop at 10. Skill level 8 opens the first specialty slot. Each stop point lists the upgrades that opened since the last one, in priority order. Respec is free until Lv 31.
