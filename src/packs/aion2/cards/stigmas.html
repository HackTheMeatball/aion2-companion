    How shards work

    Shards per level, the same for every stigma: levels 1–5 cost 1 each, 6–10 cost 2, 11–15 cost 4, 16–20 cost 8 (75 to reach 20). Learning a stigma is its level 1. Bonus effects switch on at 5, 10, 15 and 20; past 20 needs Superior Stigma Shards. You get 1 shard per character level from 23 to 39 (2 from 40); the card adds them when you level up.
