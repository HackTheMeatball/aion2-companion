Sync levels

Type each skill's level from its tooltip title, without the green bonus: "Heart Gore Lv. 6 (+1)" → 6. The number on the skill icon includes board bonuses (7), so don't use that one. Then apply. Skill lines in the milestones get ticked or unticked to match.

How points work

You earn points two ways: from character levels (24 by Lv 12, 28 by Lv 13, up to 203 at Lv 45), and 1 per Wisdom Stone you use from your Cube. Enter your stones used above and the card works out what you can spend. The skill window's points breakdown shows both and how many are unspent. Levels 2–4 cost 1 point, 5–7 cost 2, 8–10 cost 4; points stop at 10. Skill level 8 opens the first specialty slot, 12 the second and 20 the third; the skill's Daevanion nodes count toward that level (+1 each). Each milestone lists the core upgrades that opened since the last one, in priority order; the extra skills after them are in this card only. Respec is free while you level; check the reset button's cost at 31.
