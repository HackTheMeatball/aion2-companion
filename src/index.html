      The map

      Lv 1–9: Ishalgen, with Aldelle Village as the story hub. No regional quests.

      Lv 10–45: Altgard. 60 one-time Sealed Dungeons, 58 regional quests and 15 Strongholds, all tickable in Dungeons & Strongholds.

      Skills

      Sync levels

      Type each skill's level from its tooltip title, without the green bonus: "Heart Gore Lv. 6 (+1)" → 6. The number on the skill icon includes board bonuses (7), so don't use that one. Then apply. Skill lines in the milestones get ticked or unticked to match.

      How points work

      You earn points two ways: from character levels (24 by Lv 12, 28 by Lv 13, up to 203 at Lv 45), and 1 per Wisdom Stone you use from your Cube. Enter your stones used above and the card works out what you can spend. The skill window's points breakdown shows both and how many are unspent. Levels 2–4 cost 1 point, 5–7 cost 2, 8–10 cost 4; points stop at 10. Skill level 8 opens the first specialty slot. Each milestone lists the core upgrades that opened since the last one, in priority order; the extra skills after them are in this card only. Respec is free while you level; check the reset button's cost at 31.

        How shards work

        Shards per level, the same for every stigma: levels 1–5 cost 1 each, 6–10 cost 2, 11–15 cost 4, 16–20 cost 8 (75 to reach 20). Learning a stigma is its level 1. Bonus effects switch on at 5, 10, 15 and 20; past 20 needs Superior Stigma Shards. You get 1 shard per character level from 23 to 39 (2 from 40); the card adds them when you level up. Plan: Triniel's Dagger to 10, then Illusive Clone toward 20 (GamesFuze, Skycoach); the others stay at 1. Costs from Metabot and allthings.how, not yet checked in game.

            Key setup
            1. Put the macro on right click: Esc → Key Settings → General → Gameplay → Macro, press right click and confirm moving it from its current key (only in the current control mode, not AION 1 mode).
            2. Make the macro: K → Macro (top right) → Add Macro. Step 1 = the right-click macro slot (the right-click stack). If ready skills get skipped, add a short delay.
            3. Hold right and left click together in a fight. Right click alone drains MP; holding left click too keeps it full (your test).

            Without the macro, each click fires one skill from the stack, so you'd have to keep clicking. Press K to open this grid in game and put each skill in the row shown. Row 0 is tried first; anything on cooldown or not ready is skipped. Quick Slice stays alone on left click: with no cooldown it would block everything above it in a stack.

            Right click fires the first ready skill, row 0 first, so Heart Gore jumps in whenever a crit opens it. Left click (Quick Slice) restores the MP Savage Roar spends.

            Row order and the E stack are the guide's inference (Heart Gore first because it only opens after a crit; Insignia Explosion after the builders because it scales with stacks). Test on a training dummy; if a skill never fires, move it down a row.
